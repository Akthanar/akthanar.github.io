About me


Personal Background:


Hi, I'm Luca,
an Italian freelance developer, specialized in videogames.
I attended a Game Design course for three years at Event Horizon School,
followed by another two years of programming on Unity, at same school.

After those years of study, I kept studying both subjects independently,
gaining experience with small personal projects and other people's projects.
I also partecipated in several Game Jams (GGJ and various Itch.io's jams).
Currently, I'm specializing in tool programming and editor scripting on Unity.


Professional Interests:


I primarily work as gameplay / tools programmer or project manager.
I also consider positions as a game designer (not the only or the main)
and also project manager, for relatively small projects.

Occasionally, I enjoy collaborating on small free projects as support for
programmers or as consultant in game design and project management.

I also develop custom tools, for free use, not for sale, for small teams,
with the possibility of purchasing the license and the source code.
